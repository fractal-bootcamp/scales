# Capstone: LIVING SOFTWARE

**For:** Fractal Tech Cohort
**Duration:** Final 2 weeks

---

## The Brief

Build a piece of living software. Not an artifact you build once and tinker with — a system that responds to the world. Something that listens, reacts, adapts. Maybe it runs a daily cron. Maybe it processes incoming user feedback. Maybe it wakes up every morning and does something useful. The point is: your software is alive.

You decide the project. You decide solo or group. But whatever you build, it must sustain over **100 pull requests per person** across these final 2 weeks — that's 10+ PRs per day. This is a large number, and exactly the kind of thing that should be fun to do as a capstone for your agentic coding setups.

Solve a single problem extremely well. Do not boil the ocean.

---

## What You Need to Do

### Submit a Pitch

Before you start building, write a short pitch. It should cover:

- **What you're building** — a clear description of the project and the single problem it solves
- **Why it's alive** — how does your software respond to the world? What makes it living rather than static?
- **Solo or group** — and if group, who and why
- **Scope** — convince us it's tight. One problem, solved extremely well.

### Build It

Spend two weeks building. Your Claude harness should be accelerating alongside you — every PR, your setup should get better.

**All of your project-level `.claude/` and `CLAUDE.md` files must be committed in the repo as part of the project.** This is a feather in your cap — your project-level Claude setups are part of the deliverable. Show us how you work.

### Scaffold Public Writing, Brand & Voice

Your project (read: your Claude harness) must scaffold its own public writing, brand, voice, and marketing. It must talk to its own users, and make sure their feedback is being addressed.

At minimum, this means a **blog website** with email subscriptions and comments (e.g., `blog.myproject.com`). If you want to be ambitious, use other social platforms, build a wiki for the project, whatever. But the blog is the floor.

This will be super cool for employers.

### Talk to Users

Your software must have real users, and you must be responding to their feedback. The blog is one channel for this. Build others if it makes sense.

### Present Your Work

At the end of two weeks, we demo.

## The Philosophy

Week 4 asked you to build something impossible. Week 5 asked you to finish something completely. This capstone asks you to build something that **stays alive after you stop typing**.

Most software is an artifact — you build it, you deploy it, you move on. Living software is different. It has a feedback loop with the world. Users talk to it. It talks back. It changes based on what it hears. This is harder, and it's what real products do.

Since the public writing and user feedback alone is a reasonable amount of work, keep the core project tight in scope. Maybe your capstone is a much better version of an existing project you've already built. Maybe it's animating and bringing your own personal website to life, so it can collect feedback from people you meet and act as an operating system for your world. Whatever it is — one problem, solved well, kept alive.

---

## What Makes a Good Pitch

- Tight scope — you can describe the single problem in one sentence
- A clear "alive" mechanism — how it responds to the world, even if that's just a daily cron
- A plan for users — who are they, how do you reach them, how do you hear their feedback
- A plan for public writing — what does the blog look like, who is the voice
- If it builds on an existing project, a clear explanation of what's different this time

## What We Don't Want

- Ocean-boiling — "I'm going to build a platform that does everything"
- Static artifacts — something you build once and never touch again
- No users — if nobody is using it, it's not alive
- Claude setups that aren't committed — your `.claude/` config is part of the project
- Public writing that's clearly just AI slop — your brand and voice should be yours

---

## Ideas If You're Stuck

- **Rebuild and animate an existing project** — take something you've already built and make it living. Add the feedback loop. Add the public voice. Make it respond to the world.
- **Turn your personal website into an OS for your life** — a site that collects feedback from people you meet, responds to emails, publishes updates, acts as your operating system for the world.
- **Build the tool you actually need** — something small that you'll use every day, that gets better every day, that other people can use too.

---

## The Numbers

- **100+ PRs per person** over 2 weeks
- **10+ PRs per day** — this is the pace
- **1 blog** at minimum, with email subscriptions and comments
- **Real users** giving real feedback that you actually address
- **All `.claude/` and `CLAUDE.md` files** committed in the repo
